# User Manual

This manual explains how to use the People's Budget Portal to prepare, review,
endorse, and approve Annual Investment Program (AIP) submissions.

## Start here

Everyone — whatever your role — should read
**[getting-started.md](getting-started.md)** first. It covers accepting your
invitation, signing in, and finding your way around.

## Which guide is for me?

Your experience depends on your **role** and whether you propose, endorse, or
approve. Every program is now an **LGU city-office** submission; NCPC is an
**endorser body**, not a proposer.

### Roles

| Role | What it means |
|---|---|
| **Staff** | You prepare proposals for your office. |
| **Head** | You lead an office — you do everything Staff can, plus you review and forward your own office's submissions. Some Heads (and the NCPC head / endorsing-CSO reps) also endorse other offices' submissions. |
| **Administrator** | You run the portal city-wide — cycles, ceilings, users, and final approval. |

> ### ⚠️ Start here for Phase 1
>
> The portal currently runs a **publish-with-estimates** workflow, not the
> submit-and-approve chain the role guides below describe. CPDO encoders enter
> each office's AIP and publish it; offices review what was published and
> comment.
>
> **Everyone should read [phase-1-encoding.md](phase-1-encoding.md) first.** The
> guides below still describe the full approval workflow, which is paused and
> will be redesigned — each carries a notice at the top.

> Not sure of your role or track? Open the menu under your name in the top-right
> corner — it shows your office/organisation. Your Administrator set both when

### No account?

Residents can browse approved and pending-approval proposals and send feedback
without signing in — see the **[public registry citizen's guide](public-registry.md)**.
They can also build and share their own charts — see **[Explore the data](explore.md)**.
