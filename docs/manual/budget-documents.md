# Budget documents

**Who this is for:** CPDO and City Budget Office staff who check an imported
budget and enact it. You need the *View budget documents* permission to see the
page, and *Review budget documents* to mark rows reviewed or enact.

A **budget document** is one of the city's official budget papers for a year,
kept exactly as printed:

| Document | What it is |
|---|---|
| **Local Expenditure Program (LEP)** | The Mayor's proposed budget, sent to the Sangguniang Panlungsod by 16 October |
| **Appropriation Ordinance** | The budget the Sangguniang passed and the Mayor approved: the enacted Annual Budget |

The AIP is not a budget document. It stays in the **AIP Registry**.

## Find a document

Click **Budget Documents** in the top menu. Each year lists its documents with
their status:

- **Draft**: imported, still being checked. Nobody outside the portal sees it.
- **Submitted** (LEP): sent to the Sangguniang. It can no longer change.
- **Enacted** (Ordinance): the enacted budget. The public site and open data
  show it, and it can no longer change.

## Read a document

Open a document to see five tabs:

| Tab | Shows |
|---|---|
| **By PPA** | Every program, project and activity, grouped by office, in the order the book prints them. PS, MOOE and CO are each row's own money; **Total** includes the rows under it. Under each office, **Mandate, vision and mission as printed** opens that year's header for the office, with a link to the page it was read from. |
| **By object of expenditure** | Each office's money by account code and fund, as in the book's object-of-expenditure pages |
| **Import review** | Rows the import had to make a decision on |
| **Compare with the year before** | Each object and receipt line's figure for last year, as this book prints it, beside last year's own document (its Ordinance, else its LEP). Only the lines that differ are listed, largest first. A difference is not an error: the Sanggunian or a supplemental budget may have changed last year's figure |
| **History** | Who imported, reviewed and enacted the document, and when |

## Start the LEP from the AIP

Once the year's AIP is adopted, **Budget Documents** shows a **Start the FY… LEP**
button. It first asks for the **total allocable budget**: what the city can
appropriate for the year, from its estimated income and available resources.
Then it makes a draft Local Expenditure Program with every program, project and
activity of the AIP and their amounts, plus the approved Projects' (PPMP) items
by account code. A row whose money is under several funds without a split is
marked **needs review**: confirm its fund before submitting.

The draft shows how its PPAs compare with the allocable total. Going over is a
warning, not a block, and the total can be changed while the LEP is a draft.

A Project (PPMP) whose line items are linked to budget rows shows a similar
warning when the year's Projects together plan to spend more on a row than the
row's budget (the AIP amount for an Indicative PPMP, the enacted amount for a
Final one). It never stops you saving or publishing.

## Review imported rows

Documents are imported from the city's files by a developer. Some rows need a
person to look at them, for example a row printed without a code. These rows are
marked **needs review**.

1. Open the **Import review** tab.
2. Check each row against the printed book (the Ordinance, or for FY2023–2025 the
   LEP scans). Under each row is where it came
   from, for example **p. 2 · row 14**. When the source is a scan on the city
   website, clicking it opens the scan beside the list at that page; find the
   row there and compare. The list stays open and scrollable next to it, so you
   can go down the rows and click each one's reference: the scan switches to that
   row's page, and the row you are on is highlighted. **On the city portal** opens
   the same file in a new tab. Close the scan with **×** or Esc.
3. If it is right, click **Mark reviewed**.

The FY2026 Ordinance also lists the special funds' entries (SPA, LDF and
LDRRMF). For most of them the book does not say which office runs them, so
those are placed under an office from a list the City Budget Office confirms. Most LDRRMF entries
are printed as a code only; until the City Budget Office supplies their names,
they show the code as their name and are marked **needs review**.

If a row is wrong, do not mark it reviewed. Ask a developer to fix the file and
import it again. A draft can be imported again as often as needed.

FY2023–2025 were read from scans by a computer (OCR). Amounts are checked
automatically: a row's PS, MOOE and CO must add up to its total, and an office's
rows to its printed total, so a row flagged for money is one where that failed.
Names have no such check, so look out for dropped spaces and swapped letters
too. Which sheets did not add up is listed in the year's import report.

## Submit a past year's LEP

The city's website has only the proposed budget (LEP) for FY2023, 2024 and 2025, not the
enacted ordinance. Those years are imported as LEPs. Once you submit one, it appears on the
public Annual Budget page as that year's **Proposed Budget**, marked "LEP".

1. Review every row in **Import review** against the scan, as for an ordinance. Submitting is
   refused while any row still needs review.
2. Click **Submit this LEP**.

When the enacted ordinance for that year arrives, it is imported beside the LEP and replaces
it on the public page once enacted.

## Enact the Ordinance

Enact only after the Mayor has approved the Ordinance (signed it, or let the 10
days pass without acting).

1. Make sure **Import review** is empty. The button stays disabled until it is.
2. Click **Enact this Ordinance**, then **Enact** to confirm.

Enacting cannot be undone. The Annual Budget pages on the public site and the
open data switch to the Ordinance within a few minutes.

An LEP is finalized the same way with **Submit this LEP**, once the Mayor has
sent it to the Sangguniang.
