# A PPA in a Budget Document stores its own amount; its total is derived

In the city's books a heading row carries money of its own as well as its children's (87 of 90
class-heading rows in AB 2026 do). So each PPA in a Budget Document stores its **own** PS, MOOE, FE
and CO, and its **total** is its own amount plus its children's totals, computed by the service and
checked on import. This deliberately differs from the AIP, where a program's total is the sum of its
PAs. Forcing that rule onto the Ordinance meant inventing child rows to hold a heading's own money,
which is the workaround the first 2026 seed used. Forms still print each row's own amount on its own
line, so nothing is counted twice.
